FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about our services

What types of applications do you develop?

+

We develop mobile applications (iOS, Android, cross-platform), web applications (SaaS, PWA, enterprise), and desktop software for Windows, Mac, and Linux. Our expertise spans across all major platforms and technologies.

How long does it take to complete a project?

+

Project timelines vary based on complexity and scope. A simple mobile app might take 2-3 months, while complex enterprise solutions can take 6-12 months. We provide detailed timelines during our initial consultation.

What is your pricing structure?

+

We offer flexible pricing models including fixed-price projects, hourly rates, and retainer agreements. Pricing depends on project requirements, technology stack, and timeline. Contact us for a custom quote.

Do you provide ongoing support and maintenance?

+

Yes, we offer comprehensive maintenance and support packages to ensure your applications run smoothly. This includes bug fixes, updates, security patches, and feature enhancements as needed.

What technologies do you use?

+

We use modern technologies including React, Vue, Angular for web; React Native and Flutter for mobile; Electron for desktop; and various backend technologies like Node.js, Python, and .NET. We choose the best tools for each project.
